# Project Assets & Textures (`src/assets/textures/`)

All graphical assets in the project are organized into clean, categorized subfolders:

### 👤 Characters (`characters/`)
- `char_front.png`, `char_back.png`, `char_left.png`, `char_right.png`: Sitting NPC sprites ("char") in 4 orientations (and 64x96 versions).
- `character-f.png`, `character-l.png`, `character-r.png`: Player portrait/face status icons for the HUD bar.
- `character-walk.png`, `character-run.png`: Player movement stance icons (walk/sprint).

### 🖥️ Accessories & Props (`accessories/`)
- `monitor_front.png`, `monitor_back.png`, `monitor_left.png`, `monitor_right.png`: PC Monitor desk accessory in 4 orientations.
- `monitor.png`, `monitor.jpg`, `monitor.b64.txt`: Complementary textures and data.

### 🧱 Walls (`walls/` - 64x192 px)
- `castle.png`: Seamless dungeon stone wall.
- `white.png`: Seamless minimalist light panel.
- `black.png`: Dark matte panel.
- `crystal.png`: Translucent glass panel with specular glints.
- `crystal-c.png`: Framed glass variant with transparency.
- `grass-1.png`, `grass-2.png`, `pink.png`, `pink-1.png`, `pink-2.png`: Thematic custom surfaces.

### 🚪 Doors (`doors/` - 64x128 px)
- `castle.png`: Dungeon wooden door with golden handle.
- `white.png`: Clean modern door with silver handle.
- `black.png`: Dark door with dark handle.
- `crystal.png`: Glass translucent door.

### 🪟 Windows (`windows/` - 64x192 px)
- `window.png`: Architectural window panel with glass and sill.

### 📐 Jambs & Caps (`caps/` - 64x192 px)
- `castle.png`, `white.png`, `black.png`, `crystal.png`, `crystal-c.png`, etc.: Matching frame jambs for wall corners and door frames.

### 🎨 Custom (`custom/`)
- Desk components (`mesa-l.png`, `mesa-t.png`, etc.) and user-uploaded textures from the Editor.
